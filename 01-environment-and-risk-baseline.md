# 01 — Establishing the Defensive Baseline

The simulation began with limited staff and budget, which forced prioritization rather than checklist security. Early work included asset inventory, vulnerability mapping, log collection/analysis, security awareness, firewall/security controls, patching, antivirus, stronger Wi-Fi, and hardening.

## Threat-Hunting Takeaway
Before hunting effectively, you need visibility and context. Asset inventory tells you what exists; vulnerability mapping shows where exposure may exist; centralized logging helps reveal what those systems are actually doing.

## Analyst Decision Pattern
I treated each turn as a risk decision: identify the exposed asset, understand its role, determine whether the control was safe to deploy, and prioritize actions that improved visibility or removed the most exploitable weakness.

## Interview Talking Point
"The simulator reinforced that threat hunting starts before the query. If I do not understand the environment, critical assets, expected behavior, and available telemetry, I cannot reliably distinguish normal activity from meaningful anomalies."
